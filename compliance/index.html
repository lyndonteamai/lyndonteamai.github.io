Employee Compliance Training and Audit Reporting

Compliance training uses assigned learning and assessments to support obligations your organization has identified. Record the audience, course version, due date, result, and exception decision so reviewers can trace what happened.

Can an LMS prove that our company meets a regulatory requirement?

An LMS can provide evidence such as assignments, completion, assessment outcomes, and configured report exports. Ask your compliance owner to confirm the applicable requirement, the evidence they accept, and how long records need to be kept.

How should we track overdue or renewed training?

Agree the reminder, escalation, and reassignment rules first. Test one overdue assignment and one recertification case, then verify the audit export.
