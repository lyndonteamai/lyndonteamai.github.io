Moodle roles and permissions for Hong Kong teams

Design access around learning responsibilities and verify it with real scenarios.

01

Context-based permissions

Moodle roles combine capabilities in system, category, course or activity contexts. Use the least access required for each responsibility.

02

Enrolment and cohorts

Choose enrolment methods and learner groupings to match your onboarding and course allocation process.

03

Language and course administration

Agree who may edit content, manage participants, review grades and access reports.

04

Separate groups from tenants

Groups and cohorts organize participation; they do not automatically provide independent tenant isolation.
