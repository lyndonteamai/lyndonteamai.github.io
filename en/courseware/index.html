The platform is the easy part. Good courseware is where learning outcomes are won or lost. Our team designs and produces Hong Kong-specific training content — in English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese — that plugs directly into the platform.

Moodle Core vs. Configured Workflows and Extensions

This Astro website describes our Moodle-based service offering. The six solution areas are not six native Moodle modules. HRIS/SSO, sponsorship approvals, xAPI/LRS, certificates, and specialized reporting require configuration, compatible extensions, or scoped development. A working Moodle installation and acceptance evidence must be verified for your implementation. View the published Pricing page for subscription rates and separate at-cost infrastructure charges.

Hong Kong eLearning Courseware: Key Facts and Implementation Scope

What it is
Bespoke courseware development: instructional design, content production and packaging for Hong Kong organizations and schools.
Output formats
Moodle core supports SCORM 1.2 and H5P. SCORM 2004 is not supported natively. xAPI/LRS connections and PDF certificates require compatible extensions or integration, validated for the selected Moodle version.
Languages
English, Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong) and Simplified Chinese, from one design brief.
Typical programs
Compliance and policy training, onboarding, leadership development, technical and safety curricula.
Commercial model
This is a scoped, quoted service. It is the work that has to be done regardless of which platform you own.

Hong Kong eLearning Courseware: Frequently Asked Questions

What deliverables do you produce?

Instructional design, then production: the course structure and assessment plan, the learning content, interactive H5P activities, SCORM 1.2 or 2004 packaging, question banks and randomized exam papers. In English, Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong) and Simplified Chinese, from one course model so the three versions stay in step.

Who owns the content you produce?

You do. The courseware is commissioned work, and the source files, packages and question banks are yours. We are not licensing it back to you, and we do not reuse your content in other clients' material without your written permission.

How long does a course take to produce?

It varies with scope, and we will not quote a number before we understand the subject, the audience and the assessment requirement. What we do is tell you the range, the assumptions behind it, and the milestone structure, so you can hold the schedule to something rather than to a date in an email.

Do you work with our subject-matter experts?

Yes, and it is usually where the quality comes from. The typical pattern is that your SME owns the accuracy of the content and we own the instructional design, the production and the packaging. We will tell you clearly when a topic needs a review cycle, because that is where schedules slip.

Can you rebuild content that was authored in a proprietary tool?

Moodle core supports SCORM 1.2 and H5P. SCORM 2004 is not supported natively. xAPI/LRS connections and PDF certificates require compatible extensions or integration, validated for the selected Moodle version.

Do you produce content for the platform, or is it platform-agnostic?

Platform-agnostic by design, with the platform as the default delivery target. Standards-based packages (SCORM, H5P, xAPI) mean the content survives a change of platform, which is precisely the point of owning it rather than renting it.
