Moodle LMS Deployment in Hong Kong: Cloud, On-Premises, and Managed Service

Deployment is the decision that determines your recurring cost and your data residency. It is also the decision most often deferred. Here are the four realistic options, what each actually costs, and what each commits you to.

Moodle Core vs. Configured Workflows and Extensions

This Astro website describes our Moodle-based service offering. The six solution areas are not six native Moodle modules. HRIS/SSO, sponsorship approvals, xAPI/LRS, certificates, and specialized reporting require configuration, compatible extensions, or scoped development. A working Moodle installation and acceptance evidence must be verified for your implementation. View the published Pricing page for subscription rates and separate at-cost infrastructure charges.

Moodle Hosting, Maintenance, and Recovery Responsibilities

Managed hosting
We maintain Moodle and support the hosted platform. Subscriptions follow published Standard or Scale rates; cloud infrastructure is passed through at cost. Agree on data location, availability, backup retention, and recovery targets.
Your cloud or on-premises environment
The license is HK$0. Standard installation is HK$30,000. Define network access, identity provider, user import, operating ownership, and handover requirements.
Security and recovery
Maintain a supported Moodle release and compatible plugins. Use least-privilege access, scheduled backups, staging tests, and practiced restoration. Data location alone does not establish compliance.

Hong Kong Moodle Deployment: Frequently Asked Questions

Can the data stay in Hong Kong?

Yes. A managed deployment can be hosted in Hong Kong data centers, and on-premises or private cloud options keep the data inside your own boundary. We can also support the data-residency arrangements the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance requires for your specific case.

Do examinations really work with no internet?

Yes. Examination computers can connect over a private local network without public internet during the session. The exam module supports isolated workstations, with controlled loading of exam materials and collection of results — which is what makes it usable for high-security and regulated delivery.

What does "managed operations" actually include?

Patching, core and module upgrades, monitoring, backup verification, incident response, and the administration needed to keep the platform configured as your organization changes. It is scoped as a retainer with defined responsibilities, and it is separate from the license, which remains HK$0.

Can we start on-premises and move to cloud later?

It is possible, but moving an active training platform with real history is a migration, not a copy. If you expect the deployment model to change, that changes which one you should start with — we would rather flag it at the start than after the first cohort has enrolled.

Who owns the data and the code?

You do, in every deployment. The platform is GPL open source, the data lives in your environment in a standard schema, and there is no export fee to negotiate on the way out.

Is there a lock-in risk in the managed option?

The operational knowledge sits with us, which is a real dependency, and we will not pretend otherwise. The mitigations are that the code and data are yours, the configuration is documented, and we hand over runbooks. If you want to bring operations in-house later, that is a supported transition rather than an exit.

Plan Your Moodle Deployment Project in Hong Kong

Data residency, existing infrastructure, who is available to run it, and what you can afford annually. We will tell you which of the four options fits — and if none of them does, we will say that.
