Key facts

What it is
The enterprise platform layer beneath the six modules: multi-tenancy, role-based access, workflow, reporting, APIs and deployment options.
Built on
Drupal 10.3+ / Drupal 11 and the Drupal LMS ecosystem, forked from Opigno and rewritten for Drupal 10.
Access control
Role-based access control with audit history, on the same records used for training.
Deployment
On-premise, private cloud or managed hosting.
Licence cost
HK$0. No vendor contract, no renewal, no seat count.

Planning your implementation

What your team should prepare

Start with the training processes and systems you need to connect. This checklist helps HR, learning teams and IT define a useful scope before agreeing configuration, integrations and rollout.

  • Choose the first learning activities — which of internal, external, online or micro learning comes first, and which employee groups need access
  • Map people and permissions — department structure, manager relationships, administrator roles, and examples of who should see each type of record
  • Document approvals and claims — the rules, review stages, exceptions and supporting documents needed for your training workflows
  • Review integration and existing records — HR and identity systems, fields to exchange, historical records to carry over
  • Agree deployment and reporting requirements — cloud or on-premise responsibilities with IT, and examples of the reports managers need

Questions about implementation

Which HR and login systems can be integrated, and how do cloud and on-premise differ?

Which HR and login systems can OpenLMS integrate with?

The platform supports configured HRIS data exchange, single sign-on and API-based data flows. Specific connections depend on your source systems and agreed implementation. Bring the system names, authentication requirements, fields and update requirements so the team can confirm the integration approach.

How do cloud and on-premise deployment differ?

The choice affects where the platform runs and who is responsible for its infrastructure. Both options are supported. Agree hosting location, access, backup arrangements, maintenance and support responsibilities before selecting a model.

How much does implementation cost, and how long does it take?

The platform is HK$0. On-premise installation is a fixed HK$30,000 covering organisation structure, user import, SSO configuration and go-live training. Anything beyond that standard scope is customisation at HK$1,500 per man-day, estimated before work starts. Timeline depends on how much configuration your processes need — bring your modules, employee groups, workflows, integrations and existing records to the discussion so we can size it properly.

What happens to our data if the project ends?

Your data remains in a standard relational schema that you control. There is no proprietary export gate and no licence to lose. Custom development lives in your own repository.
