Questions we get asked about Hong Kong open-source LMS

Is there really a free open-source LMS that a Hong Kong school can use?

Moodle core supports SCORM 1.2 and H5P. SCORM 2004 is not supported natively. xAPI/LRS connections and PDF certificates require compatible extensions or integration, validated for the selected Moodle version.

How does the Moodle foundation affect implementation?

OpenLMS is based on Moodle, a GPL-licensed learning management system. Moodle core provides courses, activities, quizzes, grades and learning records. Additional workflows require configuration, compatible plugins or scoped development.

How much does an open-source LMS cost in Hong Kong?

The license is HK$0. What remains is hosting, configuration, integration, content development and administration. For a small organization a commercial license can still be cheaper in total; for larger organizations with existing technical capability, open source is usually substantially cheaper over three years.

Which content standards should a Hong Kong e-Learning team support?

SCORM 1.2 (SCORM 2004 is not native to Moodle) for packaged and legacy content, H5P for browser-authored interactive activities with no authoring license cost, and xAPI for recording learning that happens outside a course — workplace training, meetings or supervisor coaching.
