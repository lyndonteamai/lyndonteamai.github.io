The scope behind the six solution areas

Internal training, external training, online learning, microlearning, exam management, and reporting are included without module fees. These are solution areas, not six native Moodle modules. Moodle core provides SCORM 1.2 and H5P; xAPI/LRS, PDF certificates, specialized workflows, and integrations require compatible extensions, configuration, or scoped development. Inclusion does not mean unlimited custom development.

Why the price is low

The license isn’t the product. The work is.
Moodle is GPL-licensed software. We choose to charge HK$0 for its software license and publish our service rates. Open-source licenses permit commercial services and preserve your rights under their terms.
Where our margin goes
Our commitment is to reinvest implementation and custom development profit in open-source engineering, maintenance, documentation, and community improvements. Cloud infrastructure is passed through at cost. This is an operating commitment, not a claim of completed donations.
Your agreement
A service agreement defines delivery and operational responsibilities. Your organization controls its records; Moodle and extensions retain their applicable licenses and copyright notices.

Pricing questions, answered

Is HK$30 per user per year the total cost?

It is the Standard managed-service subscription rate. Cloud infrastructure is separate and billed at cost without markup. A 100-user subscription is HK$3,000 per year, plus infrastructure. Optional installation or custom development uses the published rates and an agreed scope.

What counts as one user?

A named user is one unique person assigned an account in the managed subscription, rather than a concurrent login. Confirm the billable account list and treatment of inactive accounts in the service agreement before billing begins. Self-managed software has no license seat limit.

How does the 500-user threshold work?

Standard applies below 500 named users. At 500 or more, Scale applies to every user, not just users above the threshold. For example, 500 users cost HK$10,000 per year in subscription fees, plus at-cost infrastructure. Confirm user-count changes and billing adjustments in the service agreement.

What does the HK$30,000 on-premises installation include?

The fixed fee covers the agreed standard installation, organization structure and user import, SSO setup, launch training, and handover. We confirm prerequisites, import scope, identity provider compatibility, and acceptance criteria before starting. Custom connectors, complex data cleanup, or other work beyond standard setup is estimated at HK$1,500 per person-day.

Why price custom development by the day instead of by project?

A published person-day rate makes the estimate easy to check: agreed development days × HK$1,500. We define the work and estimated effort before starting and agree on scope changes before additional work. One person-day means one developer’s working day; the service agreement defines working hours.

Is the platform really free for everyone?

Yes. The self-managed software offering has a HK$0 license fee, with all six solution areas and no software seat limit or expiration. Running it still requires infrastructure, maintenance, and administration. Moodle and extensions keep their applicable GPL or other open-source license notices. Optional hosted and implementation services are paid services.

What happens if we stop paying?

Your rights under the applicable open-source licenses continue, and your learning records remain under your control. Standard-format data export has no exit fee. Managed hosting, maintenance, and support end according to the service agreement; agree on export timing and a transition plan before service ends.

Will the prices stay this low forever?

These are our published current rates. We do not promise that paid-service prices can never change. Any future rate, renewal, or notice terms must be stated in your service agreement. Rights already granted under the applicable open-source software licenses remain subject to those licenses.

What does reinvesting profit in open source mean?

Our commitment is to put implementation and custom development profit back into open-source engineering, maintenance, documentation, and improvements. This is an operating commitment, not a claim that a donation has already been made or that every extension is part of Moodle core.
