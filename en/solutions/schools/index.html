For schools

Course delivery and assessment without per-student licence cost

Built on Drupal and designed for Hong Kong schools, tutoring centres and course providers. Unlimited student accounts, teacher-authored content, structured assessment, and Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong) as a first-class interface language.

Key facts

Who it is for
Hong Kong schools, tutoring centres, academies and course providers, including students, parents, teachers and administrators.
What it covers
Structured course delivery, H5P interactive content, continuous assessment, certificates, transcripts and parent communication.
Student accounts
Unlimited. Accounts are not licensed per seat, so student numbers can grow without a licence conversation.

Questions about OpenLMS for schools

What does it cost per student?

There is no per-student licence charge. Unlimited student accounts are supported on the same platform, and the platform licence is HK$0. The costs that do exist are hosting or on-premise infrastructure, configuration, courseware and administration — and we quote them as separate lines, because a per-student number would misrepresent where the money goes.

Can teachers author content themselves?

Yes. H5P authoring runs in the browser, so interactive content — video with interaction points, quizzes, presentations, branching scenarios — can be built by school staff without a development toolchain. That is usually the difference between a content library that grows and one that stalls after the initial set.

Can examinations run on the school network?

Yes. Examination computers can operate over a private local network with no public internet during the session, with isolated workstations, controlled exam loading and result collection. This is often a requirement for internal school assessments rather than an optional extra.

What about parent and teacher communication?

Teacher-facing views cover class enrolment, assignment, progress and marking, and certificates and transcripts are produced as PDF. Parent communication is a workflow rather than a built-in messaging product, and we scope it as configuration against whatever channel you already use.

Is the QEF Smart Education School Grant relevant to us?

Potentially, and it needs to be checked against your own situation. The grant provides a one-off HK$500,000 per school and is focused on AI in education, so it is aimed at AI-enablement projects rather than at general LMS purchase. It has to be applied for against a specific proposal, not against a software product, and eligibility is assessed by the funding body. We can structure a scope that is ready to be assessed, but we cannot tell you that you will be awarded it.

Will this work for a small school with limited technical support?

It can, but it depends on who runs the platform. Managed hosting is usually the sensible answer for a small school, and our scoping includes being honest about whether your internal capacity is enough to run it yourselves. We will not recommend self-hosting to an organisation that nobody can maintain.
