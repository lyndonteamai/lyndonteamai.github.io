Hong Kong Moodle LMS: Key Facts and Implementation Scope

What it is
A Learning Management System (LMS) and Training Management System (TMS) for Hong Kong organizations.
Built on
OpenLMS is based on Moodle, a GPL-licensed learning management system. Moodle core provides courses, activities, quizzes, grades and learning records. Additional workflows require configuration, compatible plugins or scoped development.
Platform cost
HK$0 for every customer, every module, permanently. No seat cap, no trial, no tiers.
Published prices
Hosted: HK$30 per user per year under 500 users, HK$20 at 500 and above. On-premises install: HK$30,000. Customization: HK$1,500 per person-day.
Who it serves
Corporate HR, L&D and training teams; school administrators, teachers and course providers.
Deployment
On-premises, private cloud, or managed hosting. Examination delivery can run on isolated networks.
Where our profit goes
Into building more open-source software — the platform, the modules, the documentation and the translations.
Languages
English, Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong), Simplified Chinese (Mainland).

Hong Kong Moodle LMS: Frequently Asked Questions

What does "HK$0 license fee" actually mean?

It means the platform license costs nothing. The core runs on GPL open-source code with no per-seat, per-module or annual renewal charge. It does not mean the total cost is zero: hosting, configuration, integration, content migration and the administration hours all still exist, and we quote them as separate, scoped lines before you commit to anything.

Is OpenLMS something we buy, or a project you run?

Both, in a specific sense. The platform is something you own outright once it is deployed — code, data and configuration sit in your infrastructure or your chosen hosting. The work of getting it there, and the content, is what we do for you. We are not reselling a license and we do not hold your data hostage to a renewal.

Do we have to change how our training works?

No. We map your existing process onto the modules and configure the platform around it. A successful deployment usually looks like your own forms, approval chain and reports, running on the platform — not like the platform with your organization forced into it.

Can it run on-premises instead of in the cloud?

Yes. On-premises, private cloud and managed hosting are all supported. On-premises is frequently the right choice where employee or student records need to stay inside your own network under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance. Examination delivery can also run on isolated networks with no public internet.

What happens if we stop working with you?

The site keeps running. You keep the code, the data and the configuration, and you can export everything in standard formats. Custom development lives in your own repository. We would rather be useful than unavoidable — that is a deliberate consequence of not charging you a license.
