Questions about integrations

Specific connections depend on your source systems and agreed implementation scope.

Which HR systems can be integrated?

The platform supports configured HRIS data exchange, single sign-on and API-based data flows. Bring the system names, authentication requirements, fields and update requirements so the team can confirm the integration approach.

Do we need a commercial integration module?

Usually not. Drupal provides contributed modules for SAML, OpenID Connect, JSON:API and GraphQL. Where an enterprise connector for a specific HRIS is needed, that is a scoped piece of work rather than a recurring licence.

Can we migrate our existing courses?

Yes. The LMS Migrate module provides migration plugins and templates for migrating from Opigno. SCORM packages from other systems can be uploaded directly; structured courses can be exchanged as YAML packages.

Tell us what you need connected

Bring your HRIS name, identity provider and the reports that must keep working. We will map the data flows.
