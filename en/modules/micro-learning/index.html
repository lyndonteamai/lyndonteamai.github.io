Micro Learning — useful knowledge, without the long time commitment

OpenLMS microlearning gives employees short videos and articles they can revisit during the workday. Share focused guidance, discuss practical examples, and use viewing activity to decide which content needs attention.

Questions about microlearning at work

Use short content to answer a specific workplace question and make it easy to return to.

What is microlearning useful for?

Microlearning focuses on a small, specific learning need. Examples include a short process demonstration, a policy reminder or a practical tip. OpenLMS supports videos and articles with comments and discussion so employees can exchange examples around the content.

How can we understand whether employees use the content?

Review video views over time and engagement across teams to see which items attract attention and which may need updating. Combine views with questions, feedback or workplace observations.

How does microlearning fit with longer courses?

Use focused items to reinforce a topic or provide guidance employees need to revisit. Use structured online courses when the objective requires a sequence of modules and assessed completion.

Can microlearning be mandatory and tracked for compliance?

Yes. Microlearning items can be assigned like any other activity with a due date, a completion requirement and a record in the employee learning profile — useful for policy acknowledgements and mandatory briefings.
