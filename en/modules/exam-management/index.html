Questions about exam management

Exam management for professional licensing and compliance, from a Hong Kong software provider.

Can examinations run without public internet access?

Yes. Examination computers can connect over a private local network without public internet during the session. The Exam Management module also supports isolated workstations, with controlled loading of exam materials and collection of results. The deployment approach depends on your examination centre’s infrastructure.

How does the module manage rooms, seats and computers?

Teams can plan examination sessions and room capacities, assign candidates to seats and computers, and review room timetables alongside seating plans. Workstation and exam status help staff monitor arrangements during a session.

Can exam papers use randomised questions?

Yes. Teams can prepare question banks and randomised exam papers, set time limits and passing requirements, then manage marking, result approval and release within the examination process.

Can exam results feed into employee learning records?

Yes. Because the exam module shares the platform data model, results and certificates can be written to the candidate’s learning profile, alongside sponsored external training and internal courses.
