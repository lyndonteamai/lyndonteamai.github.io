Moodle Core vs. Configured Workflows and Extensions

This Astro website describes our Moodle-based service offering. The six solution areas are not six native Moodle modules. HRIS/SSO, sponsorship approvals, xAPI/LRS, certificates, and specialized reporting require configuration, compatible extensions, or scoped development. A working Moodle installation and acceptance evidence must be verified for your implementation. View the published Pricing page for subscription rates and separate at-cost infrastructure charges.

Moodle Training Solutions: Key Facts and Implementation Scope

What this covers
Six connected modules: Internal Training, External Training, Online Learning, Micro Learning, Exam Management, and the enterprise platform behind them.
Shared data model
One employee record, course catalog, approval history and reporting layer serves all six modules — no re-entry across systems.
License cost
HK$0. No per-seat, per-module or annual renewal fee.
Content standards
Moodle core supports SCORM 1.2 and H5P. SCORM 2004 is not supported natively. xAPI/LRS connections and PDF certificates require compatible extensions or integration, validated for the selected Moodle version.
Who uses it
Corporate HR, L&D and training teams; school administrators, teachers, tutors and course providers.

Moodle Training Solutions: Frequently Asked Questions

Which module should we start with?

The one that fixes your most painful process. For most enterprises that is internal training, because enrollment and attendance chaos is what staff and managers feel first. For schools it is usually online learning or exam management. Modules share employee, course and approval data, so starting with one does not lock out the others later.

Can we add modules later?

Yes. The rollout is deliberately modular: you can start with what you need today and add capabilities as programs grow, because the shared data model is already in place from the beginning.

Do the modules actually share data, or are they separate products?

They share data. A person is one person across internal training, external training, online learning, micro learning and exams. That is the practical difference between a training system and a collection of tools, and it is what makes reporting across programs possible.

Is exam management the most complicated module?

It is the most operationally demanding, because it involves rooms, seats, question banks, randomized papers, invigilation and results. It is also the one that most often cannot use the public internet during a session. We scope it separately for that reason.

Is reporting included, or an add-on?

Reporting and insights is one of the six modules, not an add-on. It covers demand analysis, effectiveness, custom dashboards, exports, and the compliance and audit trails your governance review will ask for.
