Training support that follows your policy

Rules by role, grade, department, location or entitlement

Each employee sees only the sponsorship items they can apply for before they submit an external training request. The policy is configuration, not custom code.

  • Manager grade — transportation, examination and overseas training fees available
  • Assistant grade — transportation only; examination and overseas fees not eligible
  • Eligibility resolved from HR attributes, not asked twice
  • Changes to policy take effect immediately for new requests

Claim controls

Claims can include eligibility rules, reimbursement limits, supporting documents and approval history.

  • Receipt capture attached to the original request
  • Reimbursement limits enforced per entitlement
  • Full approval history retained for audit
  • Spend visible before commitment, not after

Questions about external training and sponsorship

Keep the decision to fund training connected to the evidence of completion.

What information can an external training request include?

Employees can provide course and provider details, fees, learning objectives and supporting files. Managers, budget owners and HR can review this information alongside the requested sponsorship.

Can sponsorship eligibility differ between employees?

Yes. Sponsorship rules apply by role, grade, department, location or entitlement. Configure the eligible items and review stages around your organisation’s policy. The amounts and roles shown in a preview illustrate a workflow, not a standard allowance.

How are claims linked to the original training request?

Employees add receipts, certificates and completion information after the course. These documents stay with the training record and claims route through the agreed approval process.

Does this work for professional licence renewal courses?

Yes. Where a professional body requires a certificate of attendance, the certificate is stored against the same record that carries the sponsorship decision, so renewal tracking and training spend appear together.
