Flexible ways to take attendance for different training scenarios

Whether the session is held in a classroom, office, training room or remote environment, OpenLMS supports multiple attendance methods so HR can close records accurately.

  • Generate and scan QR code — create a QR code for the session and let employees scan it to mark attendance
  • Link to staff app — employees confirm attendance through the staff app when the organisation enables app access
  • Web portal — training administrators manage and update attendance directly from the web portal
  • Staff card — use an employee staff card to verify attendance quickly at reception, classroom or venue check-in

Questions about internal training management

Plan company-run courses and keep attendance connected to employee learning records.

What can HR manage before a training session?

Set course categories, schedules, venues, trainers, capacity and target audiences. Manage enrolment, waitlists, reminders and participant status from the same course workflow.

How can attendance be recorded?

OpenLMS supports session QR codes, staff-card check-in, the web portal, and staff-app access where enabled. Agree the methods available for your environment and use the portal to review attendance before reporting.

What happens after the course?

Keep attendance and completion information with employee learning records, then review participation by course or department. Reminders and follow-up for outstanding actions are handled by the training operations layer.

Can we handle high-volume enrolment for large workforces?

Yes. Enrolment rules, waitlists and reminders are driven by organisational structure and group membership rather than manual lists, so a session with hundreds of participants is managed the same way as a small one.
