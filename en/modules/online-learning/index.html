Interactive content types, powered by H5P

Ten activity families out of the box

The Drupal LMS H5P module gives access to the open-source H5P activity library — no proprietary authoring licence required.

  • Interactive video with in-video questions
  • Multiple choice and true/false
  • Timeline and drag-the-words
  • Image juxtaposition and fill in the blanks
  • Crossword and drag and drop
  • Flashcards and presentations

Turn viewing into active learning

Insert questions at key moments in a video, pause playback for a response, and continue only after the learner has engaged. Results are saved to the learner record for completion and reporting.

  • Timed knowledge checks
  • Automatic response tracking
  • Completion and score records
  • Pass-mark thresholds per assessment

Structured digital learning, one focused module at a time

Build each course in clear layers: organise related content into sections, then add focused modules within every section. Employees always know what to do next, while HR can see where support is needed.

01

Questions about online learning and assessments

Choose content, assignments and assessment requirements that fit the learning objective.

What types of learning content can we use?

Build courses from videos, documents, activities and assessments. Interactive formats include video questions, multiple choice, timelines, drag-the-words, image comparison and fill-in-the-blanks questions.

Does OpenLMS support SCORM and external learning libraries?

Yes. The online learning module supports SCORM packages (1.2 and 2004), xAPI packages, and learning content integrations with external libraries. Confirm package versions, provider access, licensing and reporting requirements before selecting content.

How can HR assign and monitor courses?

Assign learning by employee, department, role, skill or target group, with due dates and completion rules. Review participation, scores, attempts, completion and overdue learning through reporting and insights.

When should we use online courses instead of microlearning?

Use a structured course when learners need a sequence of lessons, assessments and completion requirements. Use microlearning for a focused video, article, reminder or discussion that employees revisit during the workday.

Can learners complete courses on a phone?

Yes. Course pages are responsive, and H5P activities including interactive video work on mobile browsers. Examination delivery has separate requirements — see exam management for isolated-network delivery.
