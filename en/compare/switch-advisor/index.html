Self-assessment tool

Work out what an LMS switch would actually involve.

Three rounds of questions, then a written requirements brief you can read, copy and send — or discard. The whole thing runs in your browser. No account, no tracking, and nothing you type is transmitted anywhere.

Start the advisorMigration service

Key facts about this tool

What it does
Turns your situation into a structured requirements brief covering context, reasons, must-have features, migration needs, what is already satisfied, and what still needs technical validation.
How long it takes
Three rounds. If we already know enough after two, we will not ask you a third just to complete a form.
Where your answers go
Nowhere. The advisor is client-side code in your browser. There is no server call, no cookie and no analytics on this page.
What you get
A formatted brief in plain text that you can copy, paste into an enquiry, or keep for your own scoping.
What it is not
It is not a live demo, not a migration validation, and not an AI conversation. It is a structured way for you to state your own requirements.

LMS switch advisor

Answer what you know. Leave anything unclear blank — a brief that says "not answered" is more useful than one filled with guesses.

1Where you are now

The context that determines whether a switch is worth it at all.

2What must survive the switch

The things that make a migration fail are usually not the platform — they are the records and the reports.

3Constraints and timing (optional)

Only if you already know. Skipping this does not weaken the brief — it just leaves these points marked as unanswered.

What happens after you generate the brief

01
Step 1

Read it and correct it

The brief is a statement of your requirements, written by our tool from what you told it. If any part is wrong or incomplete, correct it before sending. It is your document — we are not going to interpret it for you.

02
Step 2

Send it if you want a written reply

If you choose to send it, you will get a written view on whether we think the switch makes sense, what the migration approach would be, and an honest indication of effort. If it does not make sense, we will say that too.

03
Step 3

Decide against us if it does not add up

An assessment that concludes you should stay with your current platform, or that a different vendor is a better fit, is a useful outcome. We would rather be right than be retained.

Questions about this advisor

Is anything I type sent to OpenLMS?

No. The advisor is JavaScript that runs in your browser. Your answers are not submitted, not stored, and not logged. The brief appears on your screen and you decide what to do with it.

Do you store my personal data?

Not from this tool. The enquiry form on the contact page is the only place where information reaches us, and we use what you send only to respond to that enquiry.

Do I have to complete all three rounds?

No. The third round is explicitly optional. If you leave it blank, the brief marks those points as unanswered, which is more honest than inventing an answer.

Is this a live demo of the platform?

No. It is a requirements tool. Seeing the platform configured around your process is a separate conversation, and we will offer it once we have read the brief.

Will you use my answers for marketing?

No. We do not publish customer case studies we have not been given permission to publish, and we do not quote anonymised figures either. If you want to be a reference, that is a separate and explicit agreement.

Already know what you need?

If you can state your requirements directly, skip the form and email us. A specific brief gets a better answer than a questionnaire.
