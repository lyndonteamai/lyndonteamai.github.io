About OpenLMS: Moodle LMS Services and Pricing for Hong Kong

OpenLMS is a Hong Kong open-source learning systems practice. We build and run Learning Management Systems and Training Management Systems for enterprises and schools on 100% open-source technology, we publish every price, and we put our margin back into the platform rather than into a shareholder.

See the pricesTalk to us

Moodle Core vs. Configured Workflows and Extensions

This Astro website describes our Moodle-based service offering. The six solution areas are not six native Moodle modules. HRIS/SSO, sponsorship approvals, xAPI/LRS, certificates, and specialized reporting require configuration, compatible extensions, or scoped development. A working Moodle installation and acceptance evidence must be verified for your implementation. View the published Pricing page for subscription rates and separate at-cost infrastructure charges.

Hong Kong Moodle LMS Services: Key Facts and Implementation Scope

Who we are
A Hong Kong open-source learning systems practice building and running LMS and TMS platforms for enterprises and schools.
Commercial model
The platform is free for everyone. Hosted subscription is HK$30 per user per year below 500 users or HK$20 at 500+, plus at-cost cloud infrastructure; on-premises installation is HK$30,000, and customization is HK$1,500 per person-day. All published.
Where the profit goes
OpenLMS is based on Moodle, a GPL-licensed learning management system. Moodle core provides courses, activities, quizzes, grades and learning records. Additional workflows require configuration, compatible plugins or scoped development.
Built on
Moodle open-source foundation
Coverage
English, Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong) and Simplified Chinese, as a first-class product requirement.
Principle
We will not recommend open source to an organization that cannot run it.

OpenLMS Principles for Free Moodle Software and Paid Services

01
Principle

OpenLMS Service: Software should not be a per-seat tax

Training infrastructure should be an asset you own, not an annuity you pay. Open source is the only structure that reliably delivers that.

02
Principle

OpenLMS Service: A price you cannot see is a price you cannot check

If a vendor will not publish what something costs, you cannot compare it, and you cannot budget it. So we publish ours — every number, the same for every customer, with no "contact us for pricing" behind it.

03
Principle

OpenLMS Service: Localisation is engineering, not translation

Hong Kong schools and HR teams do not need an English product with Chinese strings bolted on. Terminology, workflow and interface need to be designed for Hong Kong terminologyfirst.

04
Principle

Instructional Design and Effective eLearning Content

The platform is table stakes. If the courseware is weak, the LMS is an expensive file cabinet. That is why we do content as seriously as software.

05
Principle

Open-Source LMS Reinvestment Commitment

Our margin from installation and customization work funds engineering on the platform everyone runs. It is not a donation to a foundation with a website — it is the same engineers improving the same code you are running.

06
Principle

OpenLMS Service: Tell people when we are wrong

Open source is not always the right answer, and a platform demo should include where our product is the weaker option. We would rather lose a deal than win a bad deployment.

Hong Kong Moodle LMS Services: Frequently Asked Questions

Are you a reseller of any commercial LMS?

No. We do not resell, resell-adjacent sell or offer support contracts for any commercial LMS product, including the major Hong Kong ones. We implement and run open-source platforms, and we will happily tell you when a commercial license is genuinely the better economic choice for an organization of your size.

Where is OpenLMS based and who do you work with?

We are a Hong Kong practice, and we work with organizations across Hong Kong — corporate HR and L&D teams, school administrators, teachers and course providers. We work in English, Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong) and Simplified Chinese, and we reply in the working language you write in.

How do you make money if the platform license is free?

From custom courseware development and from implementation work. Both are needed regardless of which platform you own, which is why we can quote them competitively. It also means our incentive sits in the quality of the learning your people actually get, not in how many seats you open.

Do you guarantee the platform will work for us?

No vendor should, and we do not. What we do is scope it honestly: we tell you which of your requirements are covered by the platform as it stands, which need configuration, and which need custom development. If a requirement is a poor fit, we say so before you sign anything.

What is your position on proprietary export formats and lock-in?

Moodle core supports SCORM 1.2 and H5P. SCORM 2004 is not supported natively. xAPI/LRS connections and PDF certificates require compatible extensions or integration, validated for the selected Moodle version.
