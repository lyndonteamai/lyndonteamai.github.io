Moodle Core vs. Configured Workflows and Extensions

This Astro website describes our Moodle-based service offering. The six solution areas are not six native Moodle modules. HRIS/SSO, sponsorship approvals, xAPI/LRS, certificates, and specialized reporting require configuration, compatible extensions, or scoped development. A working Moodle installation and acceptance evidence must be verified for your implementation. View the published Pricing page for subscription rates and separate at-cost infrastructure charges.

Moodle LMS Integration: Key Facts and Implementation Scope

What it covers
Employee data, identity, learning records and content libraries connected through configured data exchange or standard APIs.
Standards
Moodle core supports SCORM 1.2 and H5P. SCORM 2004 is not supported natively. xAPI/LRS connections and PDF certificates require compatible extensions or integration, validated for the selected Moodle version.
Typical connections
HRIS, identity provider, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Slack, Teams, and external content libraries.
Data control
Your data stays in your infrastructure, in your schema, under your retention policy.
License cost
HK$0. Integration work is scoped separately and quoted before commitment.

HRIS and SSO Integration FAQs for Hong Kong Teams

How can an LMS connect with an existing HRIS?

Define identifiers, employee fields, synchronization direction, authentication and permissions. Moodle web services can support scoped interfaces; a working HRIS connector still requires development and testing.

Can Moodle connect to single sign-on?

Moodle offers authentication options that can be configured for an identity environment. Confirm the protocol, plugin compatibility, account linking and access-removal behavior.
