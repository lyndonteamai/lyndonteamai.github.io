HONG KONG PROJECT PLANNING

Questions your team may ask.

How much does an enterprise LMS cost in Hong Kong?

Total cost depends on implementation, infrastructure, migration, plugins, integrations and support. OpenLMS pricing has not been approved; request an itemized scope rather than assuming open-source software means a free service.

Does an open-source LMS eliminate hosting costs?

No. Open-source licensing does not remove infrastructure, maintenance, backups, upgrades or support costs. Compare the total scope of ownership.
