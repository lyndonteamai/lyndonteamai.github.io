OpenLMS and Karas: a Hong Kong LMS comparison guide

Compare vendor scope and demonstrated evidence before choosing an LMS.

01

Karas LMS: supplier-described scope

Karas publicly describes internal and external training, online learning, microlearning and an exam module. These are supplier descriptions, not our independent product test.

02

OpenLMS: proposed Moodle foundation

This website describes a proposed Moodle-based offering. The marketing site is Astro; a production Moodle installation and additional workflows remain to be implemented and validated.

03

Karas LMS pricing and OpenLMS costs

Request current itemized quotations from each provider. OpenLMS managed rates are HK$30 per user per year below 500 users or HK$20 at 500+, plus at-cost infrastructure. See our Pricing page; verify Karas pricing directly with that provider.

04

Compare the same proof

Ask both teams to demonstrate your courses, roles, reports, migration and support arrangements. Do not infer that an unverified capability is absent.
