Free Moodle LMS in Hong Kong: Zero License Fees and Operating Costs

The phrase gets used loosely in this market, often to mean something that is not quite what it sounds like. This page sets out precisely what a HK$0 license fee covers in our case, what it explicitly does not cover, and the questions that tell you whether another vendor’s zero-fee offer means the same thing.

Moodle Core vs. Configured Workflows and Extensions

This Astro website describes our Moodle-based service offering. The six solution areas are not six native Moodle modules. HRIS/SSO, sponsorship approvals, xAPI/LRS, certificates, and specialized reporting require configuration, compatible extensions, or scoped development. A working Moodle installation and acceptance evidence must be verified for your implementation. View the published Pricing page for subscription rates and separate at-cost infrastructure charges.

Free Moodle LMS Costs: Key Facts and Implementation Scope

What is zero
The platform license for all six modules: HK$0, with no per-seat, per-module or annual renewal charge.
What is not zero
Hosting, deployment and configuration, integration, migration, content development, and maintenance and operations. All six are real costs.
Source code
Yours under GPL, including customizations we build for you. No escrow arrangement needed because there is nothing to escrow.
The trap to avoid
A "free" open-source LMS that requires a paid authoring tool, a paid hosting tier or a paid support contract to be usable is not free. Ask which.
Our position
We do not want a customer who believed the whole thing was free. That customer is a bad fit for us and a bad experience for them.

Free Moodle Software Licensing vs. Paid Operating Services

Is there a per-seat charge?No. The number of users does not change the license fee.

Verify Free LMS Licensing, Hosting, and Service Terms

Ask four questions in writing: (1) Is the per-seat charge genuinely zero, or is it bundled into a different line? (2) Which hosted authoring or content tool do I need a paid license for? (3) What is the total cost in year one, and what changes in year three? (4) If I stopped paying you tomorrow, what would I still own, and what would I still need? A vendor with a genuine zero-license product answers all four directly. A vendor with a "free" tier that needs paid add-ons does not.

Free Moodle LMS Fit and Technical Responsibility

01
Suits

Free LMS: Organizations with technical capacity or a technical partner

If you have someone who can run a Linux server, or a partner who will, the zero license fee converts directly into money kept over three to five years. The work still has to be done, but you are buying it once instead of renting it annually.

02
Suits

Free LMS: Organizations that need to own their data and their customization

The strongest structural argument for open source is not price, it is ownership. When the platform license is zero, nothing in the architecture depends on a commercial relationship continuing. That matters for regulated training records, for groups being restructured, and for organizations that may be acquired.

03
Does not suit

Free LMS: Small organizations with no technical capacity

A one-person HR team that needs a working system in three weeks will pay more in configuration time than a commercial license would have cost. We would rather say this plainly at the start than discover it in month six of a project.

Free Moodle LMS Costs: Frequently Asked Questions

Why do you give the platform away?

Because the platform is not where we make money. Our work is custom courseware development and scoped implementation. Licensing the platform to you for nothing keeps the relationship in the area where we are genuinely useful, and it removes a procurement objection that is often the main reason a project stalls.

If the platform is free, what are you actually selling?

Three things: custom courseware built to your content and assessment design; implementation and configuration; and optional managed operations. None of these are hidden inside a license fee, because there is no license fee.

Is the source code really mine?

Yes, under the GPL, including anything we customize for you. If we ever stop working together, you keep the code, the configuration and the data. We do not use proprietary modules to create a switching cost.

Is there a paid version?

There is no paid license tier. Paid engagements are services — scoped work with deliverables, not a feature gate on the platform. The module set is the same for everyone.

Will this really be cheaper in three years?

For larger organizations with technical capability, usually yes, and the calculator on this site will let you test that with your own numbers. For a small organization with no technical capacity, it may not be. We publish the model rather than a claim precisely because the answer depends on your situation.

What if we need something you have not built?

Then it is custom work, quoted as a scoped deliverable. We will tell you whether it is better to build on the open-source stack, to use an existing third-party component, or to do something simpler. Sometimes the honest answer is that you do not need the feature.
