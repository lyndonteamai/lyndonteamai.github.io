Questions your team may ask.

Which systems combine online learning and training administration?

Compare course delivery with operational needs such as bookings, attendance, requests and reports. Moodle core covers learning activities; additional administration may require compatible plugins or integration.

How can companies record training attendance using QR codes?

Evaluate an attendance extension and QR process against session timing, identity checks and exception handling. QR attendance is not claimed as an installed OpenLMS feature.
