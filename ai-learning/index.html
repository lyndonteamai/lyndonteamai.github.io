Questions your team may ask.

How can AI recommend courses based on employee roles and skills?

Role-aware recommendations require a defined recommendation service, suitable data and human review. Do not assume Moodle AI text tools include a deployed skills-based recommendation engine.

How can learning data reveal skills gaps and training demand?

Combine assessment evidence with competency definitions and business observations. Completion totals alone do not prove competence; validate any inferred gap with responsible managers.

Can Moodle generate quizzes with AI?

Quiz-generation requirements need evaluation against the selected version and plugins. Moodle AI text-generation support does not by itself prove an automated, reviewed question-bank workflow.

What does the Moodle AI subsystem provide?

Supported versions include a framework for configured AI providers and actions such as text assistance and summaries. Availability depends on version, permissions, plugins and provider setup.
