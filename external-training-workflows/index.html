HONG KONG / MOODLE OPEN SOURCE

External training approvals and claims

Plan requests, sponsorship and reimbursement around your Hong Kong organization’s policy.

01

Capture a complete request

Collect provider, course, objectives, cost and supporting evidence. Moodle course enrolment is not a finance request workflow.

02

Agree the approval route

Define manager, budget owner and HR decisions, delegation and returned requests. Select a workflow system or custom integration.

03

Control reimbursement scope

Define eligible items, limits, receipts and review stages. Finance posting and payment tracking require explicit integration.

04

Keep the learning evidence

Link completion evidence and approved costs to the right employee. Test access, audit history and record-retention rules.

Questions your team may ask.

How can HR manage external training applications and reimbursements?

Design request, approval, completion evidence and reimbursement stages together. These finance and approval workflows need a separately evaluated plugin, business system or custom integration.

Which training systems support multi-level approval workflows?

Ask suppliers to demonstrate the required review stages, delegation and decision history. Do not assume Moodle enrolment provides a complete corporate approval and reimbursement engine.
