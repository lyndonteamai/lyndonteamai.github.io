External Course Requests, Fees, and Supporting Documents

External Training Approval Routing: Manager, HR, and Budget

External Training Workflow FAQs for Hong Kong Teams

How can HR manage external training applications and reimbursements?

Design request, approval, completion evidence and reimbursement stages together. These finance and approval workflows need a separately evaluated plugin, business system or custom integration.

Which training systems support multi-level approval workflows?

Ask suppliers to demonstrate the required review stages, delegation and decision history. Do not assume Moodle enrollment provides a complete corporate approval and reimbursement engine.
