LMS Reporting FAQs for Hong Kong Teams

How should enrollment, attendance and completion be tracked separately?

Enrollment grants course participation, attendance records presence and completion records configured learning criteria. Define and report each separately to avoid treating a check-in as proof of learning.

What reports should managers use to review training effectiveness?

Review participation, completion, assessment and application at work against the original objective. Separate operational activity from business outcomes and agree access to employee-level records.
