Moodle Core vs. Configured Workflows and Extensions

This Astro website describes our Moodle-based service offering. The six solution areas are not six native Moodle modules. HRIS/SSO, sponsorship approvals, xAPI/LRS, certificates, and specialized reporting require configuration, compatible extensions, or scoped development. A working Moodle installation and acceptance evidence must be verified for your implementation. View the published Pricing page for subscription rates and separate at-cost infrastructure charges.

Enterprise Moodle LMS: Key Facts and Implementation Scope

What it is
The enterprise platform layer beneath the six modules: multi-tenancy, role-based access, workflow, reporting, APIs and deployment options.
Built on
OpenLMS is based on Moodle, a GPL-licensed learning management system. Moodle core provides courses, activities, quizzes, grades and learning records. Additional workflows require configuration, compatible plugins or scoped development.
Access control

Moodle Platform: Planning your implementation

LMS User, Workflow, Integration, and Reporting Requirements

Start with the training processes and systems you need to connect. This checklist helps HR, learning teams and IT define a useful scope before agreeing configuration, integrations and rollout.

  • Choose the first learning activities — which of internal, external, online or micro learning comes first, and which employee groups need access
  • Map people and permissions — department structure, manager relationships, administrator roles, and examples of who should see each type of record
  • Document approvals and claims — the rules, review stages, exceptions and supporting documents needed for your training workflows
  • Review integration and existing records — HR and identity systems, fields to exchange, historical records to carry over
  • Agree deployment and reporting requirements — cloud or on-premises responsibilities with IT, and examples of the reports managers need

Enterprise Moodle LMS: Frequently Asked Questions

Which HR and login systems can be integrated, and how do cloud and on-premises differ?

Which HR and login systems can OpenLMS integrate with?

The platform supports configured HRIS data exchange, single sign-on and API-based data flows. Specific connections depend on your source systems and agreed implementation. Bring the system names, authentication requirements, fields and update requirements so the team can confirm the integration approach.

How do cloud and on-premises deployment differ?

The choice affects where the platform runs and who is responsible for its infrastructure. Both options are supported. Agree hosting location, access, backup arrangements, maintenance and support responsibilities before selecting a model.

How much does implementation cost, and how long does it take?

The platform is HK$0. On-premises installation is a fixed HK$30,000 covering organization structure, user import, SSO configuration and go-live training. Anything beyond that standard scope is customization at HK$1,500 per person-day, estimated before work starts. Timeline depends on how much configuration your processes need — bring your modules, employee groups, workflows, integrations and existing records to the discussion so we can size it properly.

What happens to our data if the project ends?

Your data remains in a standard relational schema that you control. There is no proprietary export gate and no license to lose. Custom development lives in your own repository.

Enterprise Moodle LMS FAQs for Hong Kong Teams

What is the difference between an LMS and a TMS?

An LMS focuses on learning content, activities and progress. A TMS often adds operational processes such as scheduling, venues, approvals and costs. Scope these processes separately for a Moodle project.

Is this marketing website built with Astro?

Yes. All marketing routes are statically generated by Astro. A production Moodle learning platform would run separately and is not installed by this website.
