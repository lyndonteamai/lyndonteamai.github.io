Enterprise Moodle LMS FAQs for Hong Kong Teams

What is the difference between an LMS and a TMS?

An LMS focuses on learning content, activities and progress. A TMS often adds operational processes such as scheduling, venues, approvals and costs. Scope these processes separately for a Moodle project.

Is this marketing website built with Astro?

Yes. All marketing routes are statically generated by Astro. A production Moodle learning platform would run separately and is not installed by this website.
