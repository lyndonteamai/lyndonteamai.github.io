Moodle LMS vs Workplace: Hong Kong planning guide

Understand the open-source core, optional extensions and separately licensed workplace product.

01

Moodle LMS open-source core

Courses, quizzes, assignments, H5P activities, completion, competencies, badges, groups and permissions form part of the Moodle LMS foundation, subject to version and configuration.

02

Moodle Workplace is a separate product

Workplace is available through Moodle Certified Partners and Service Providers. Its product-specific capabilities are not automatically included in a core Moodle LMS project.

03

Do not assume advanced corporate workflows

Workplace multi-tenancy, programs, recurring certifications and dynamic rules must not be represented as included Moodle LMS core capabilities.

04

Evaluate extensions explicitly

Printable certificates, attendance plugins, advanced CPD workflows and custom HR integrations need separate selection, licensing review and acceptance testing.

Source: Official Moodle documentation ↗ · Reviewed 8 November 2026. Upstream support requires project validation.

Does open-source Moodle include Workplace multi-tenancy?
