Questions your team may ask.

Can H5P activities be used in Moodle courses?

Moodle supports H5P activities and a content bank. Available content types and recorded results depend on the libraries, package and activity settings.

Are H5P completion and passing the same thing?

No. Completing interaction with H5P content, achieving a score and satisfying Moodle activity completion rules are different conditions. Check each against the reporting requirement.
