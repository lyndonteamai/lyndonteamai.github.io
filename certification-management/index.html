Questions your team may ask.

How can HR track employee certificate expiry and renewals?

Agree issue dates, expiry dates, evidence owners and renewal rules. Select and test a compatible extension or separate system; recurring certification is not assumed to be Moodle LMS core.

Can AI extract details from employee training certificates?

An OCR service can be evaluated to extract candidate fields from uploaded certificates. Human review must check accuracy; extraction does not establish authenticity. This requires additional implementation.

Are Moodle badges the same as training certificates?

Badges recognize configured achievements. Printable certificates, qualification evidence and renewal management have different requirements and may need additional plugins or services.
