Choosing a Free Moodle LMS for Your Hong Kong Organization

Evaluate free software, managed hosting and implementation responsibilities before choosing an open-source LMS.

Hong Kong Employee Learning and Assessment Requirements

List the courses, assessments, completion rules and reports your team needs. Moodle core can support common teaching and learning activities; HR sponsorship approvals, specialized examinations and identity integration need their own validation.

Free Moodle Licensing vs. Paid LMS Services

The software license is HK$0. Hosted service is HK$30 per named user per year below 500 users, or HK$20 at 500 or more users. Cloud infrastructure is billed at cost without markup. On-premises installation is HK$30,000; custom development is HK$1,500 per person-day.

Moodle Hosting and Platform Maintenance Responsibilities

A self-managed installation needs technical ownership of updates, backups, scheduled tasks, monitoring and incident response. A team without this capability should plan managed operations and agree on support, recovery and data-export responsibilities.

Moodle Feature, Plugin, and Service Validation Checklist

Moodle core supports SCORM 1.2 and H5P. SCORM 2004 is not supported natively. xAPI/LRS connections and PDF certificates require compatible extensions or integration, validated for the selected Moodle version.

Test representative learning records and reports in a staging environment. Agree on the user count, infrastructure cost and scope of any custom work using the published rates.
