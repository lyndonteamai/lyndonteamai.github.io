SCORM, xAPI, and H5P for Hong Kong Moodle eLearning Projects

Moodle core supports SCORM 1.2 and H5P. SCORM 2004 is not supported natively. xAPI/LRS connections and PDF certificates require compatible extensions or integration, validated for the selected Moodle version.

SCORM 1.2 for packaged learning

Moodle core supports SCORM 1.2. Test completion status, scores, bookmarking, and reporting with representative packages. SCORM 2004 is not supported natively; assess alternatives or integration separately.

H5P for interactive activities

Moodle includes H5P functionality. Activity types, accessibility, grading, and completion behavior vary; validate the selected content types and your Moodle version. A content file alone does not prove that every interaction is available in every context.

xAPI for events beyond the LMS

An xAPI learning record store and its connections require a compatible extension or integration. Define the events, identifiers, consent, retention, and report requirements before enabling a connection. Do not assume Moodle core automatically stores every xAPI event.

Moodle Course Backups and LMS Content Migration

Moodle course backups preserve supported course structures, while original SCORM and H5P packages may be reused where the destination supports them. Reconcile grades, learner identities, completion records, and evidence separately.

References: Moodle SCORM FAQ and Moodle H5P documentation.
