Free Moodle LMS FAQs for Hong Kong Teams

Is Moodle LMS open source?

Moodle LMS is open-source software distributed under its applicable license. Implementation services, infrastructure and third-party components have their own costs and terms.

Who owns custom Moodle development?

Agree project source delivery and rights in the contract. Existing open-source licenses and third-party copyright remain applicable; source access does not remove those obligations.
