Moodle Hosting and Deployment FAQs for Hong Kong Teams

Should a Hong Kong organization choose cloud or on-premise LMS?

Compare data location, capacity, identity access, backup, disaster recovery and maintenance ownership. Neither deployment model alone establishes security or compliance.

How should Moodle backups be planned?

Distinguish course backup from full site recovery. Define database, files, configuration, retention, restore testing and recovery responsibilities together.
