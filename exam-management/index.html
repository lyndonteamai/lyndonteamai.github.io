HONG KONG / MOODLE OPEN SOURCE

Examination planning for Hong Kong organizations

Use Moodle assessments as a foundation, with explicit boundaries for professional exam operations.

01

Prepare assessments

Design question banks, attempts, time limits and grading rules. Review randomization and assessment accessibility.

02

Plan the examination center

Eligibility, room booking, workstation allocation and supervision require separate operational tools or custom development.

03

Evaluate restricted networks

Confirm all required services are reachable in the controlled environment. Isolated computers and offline results are a separate solution.

04

Validate results and access

Test permissions, marking, result release and exception handling with exam owners. No professional-license accreditation is claimed.

Questions your team may ask.

Can professional examinations run without public internet access?

A controlled local Moodle environment may serve networked workstations without public internet when all dependencies are local. Isolated workstation delivery, loading and result collection need a separate architecture and test.
