HONG KONG / MOODLE OPEN SOURCE

Moodle migration planning in Hong Kong

Preserve the meaning of learning records as well as the data.

01

Inventory the source

Identify employees, courses, attempts, scores, completion dates and supporting evidence. Confirm what must remain accessible.

02

Clean and map

Resolve duplicate users and course names. Document field mapping, historical statuses and missing evidence.

03

Reconcile and investigate

Compare source and destination totals, inspect exceptions and sample high-priority records.

04

Obtain business acceptance

Review actual reports and permissions with record owners before launch. An import finishing is not acceptance evidence.

Questions your team may ask.

What should companies check before migrating training records?

Clean employee identifiers, define completion meanings, retain source evidence and reconcile totals. Investigate exceptions and obtain business-owner acceptance before launch.

How can managers validate migrated completion records?

Reconcile counts, identifiers, dates and completion meanings with the original source. Sample critical evidence and resolve mismatches before approving the migrated dataset.
